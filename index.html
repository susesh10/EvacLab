Simulation limitations

This simulation uses a fixed walking speed and allows occupants to overlap. It does not model crowds, smoke or reaction delays. Fire uses a fixed illustrative spread rate without material or temperature calculations. The times are estimates for this model. Follow approved procedures in real emergencies.
